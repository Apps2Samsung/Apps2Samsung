Apps2Samsung installs Jellyfin and other web and community apps onto your Samsung Tizen TV — no PC, no Tizen Studio, and no manual sideloading.

This is the mobile (phone) installer head of Apps2Samsung. Put your TV into Developer Mode, point the app at it over your local network, sign in once with a free Samsung account to provision a signing certificate, and install.

Features:

* Finds Samsung Tizen TVs on your network, or connect directly by IP.
* Installs Jellyfin (multiple build variants) and a catalog of community apps.
* Give any app a custom launcher icon and a custom title.
* Optional Jellyfin server pre-configuration: server URL, auto-login, and custom CSS.
* Uses a free Samsung account certificate — no paid developer account required.

Requirements:

* A Samsung Tizen TV in Developer Mode, on the same local network as your phone.

Desktop versions for Windows, macOS and Linux are also available. Apps2Samsung is free and open source (MIT).
