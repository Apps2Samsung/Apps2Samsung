Why Apps2Samsung

Everything the fiddly way used to need — automated.

Side-loading a Tizen app normally means Tizen Studio, certificates, and command-line tools. Apps2Samsung does all of it behind one button.

🔎

Automatic TV discovery

Scans your network and lists the Samsung TVs ready to receive an install — or type an IP manually.

🔐

Sign in to Samsung once

Generates the developer certificate for you and reuses it per TV, so you're not asked to log in on every install.

🎬

Jellyfin, ready to watch

Bake in your server URL and auto-login, apply a community theme or custom CSS, and the YouTube-trailer fix — right at install.

📋

See & manage what's installed

List the apps already on a TV at a glance and uninstall any of them in a tap.

🎨

Custom launcher icons

Give any app your own icon, applied to the package as it installs.

📺

Your own IPTV channels

Add name + m3u8 stream pairs and their play order, injected into the TVApp package.

🛟

Resilient installs

Clear, plain-language errors and automatic recovery for the common failures — no cryptic Tizen codes.

🧩

Native apps too

Installs native .tpk apps as well as web .wgt packages, plus your own custom builds.

💙

Free & open source

MIT-licensed, no accounts, no telemetry. Built in the open on GitHub.

Three steps

From download to watching, in minutes

Pick your TV

Open the app on your computer or phone. It scans your network and lists Samsung TVs in developer mode — or enter the IP yourself.

Choose an app

Select Jellyfin, Moonlight, or anything from the catalog — and the exact version. Configure Jellyfin's server and theme if you like.

Sign in once & install

Sign in to your Samsung account so the app can create the signing certificate. It's reused next time — then it installs to your TV.

Good to know

Frequently asked

Which TVs are supported?

Samsung Smart TVs, projectors and smart monitors running Tizen (roughly 2015 and newer). Older Orsay models are no longer supported — the unmaintained legacy Orsay installer remains available as-is.

Do I need a Samsung account?

Yes — Samsung's own developer flow requires signing in once so Apps2Samsung can create the signing certificate your TV needs. That certificate is then reused across installs, so you won't be asked again for the same TV.

Is this safe? Is it official?

Apps2Samsung uses Samsung's own developer/side-load mechanism — the same one Tizen Studio uses — it doesn't jailbreak or modify your TV's firmware. It's fully open source, so you can read exactly what it does. It isn't affiliated with Samsung or Jellyfin.

Do I need Developer Mode on the TV?

Yes. Enable Developer Mode on your Samsung TV (Apps → type 12345 → toggle on, set your computer/phone's IP), then scan for it in Apps2Samsung. This is the standard Tizen side-loading requirement.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It's free and MIT-licensed, with no accounts or telemetry. If it saved you time, a 🍺 coffee is always appreciated.

Can I add my own app?

Absolutely — use the Custom WGT/TPK option to install a local package. Community apps live in the open tizen-community-packages repo, and contributions are welcome.
